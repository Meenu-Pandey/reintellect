# CCTV Footage Analysis

## Store Overview

The provided CCTV footage consists of five camera feeds covering the Purplle retail store.

The objective of these cameras is to support:

* Visitor counting
* Customer journey analysis
* Queue monitoring
* Brand engagement measurement
* Security monitoring
* Conversion analytics

---

# Camera Responsibilities

## CAM1 – Checkout / POS Area

### Coverage

* Checkout counter
* Cashier station
* Customer queue area
* Product displays near checkout

### Business Purpose

* Queue monitoring
* POS occupancy tracking
* Checkout interaction analytics

### Observable Events

* Queue Join
* Queue Leave
* Queue Abandonment
* Cashier Present
* Cashier Missing

---

## CAM2 – Cosmetics / Makeup Zone

### Coverage

* Lakme
* Faces Canada
* Swiss Beauty
* Maybelline

### Business Purpose

Measure customer engagement with premium makeup brands.

### Observable Events

* Zone Entry
* Zone Exit
* Dwell Time
* Brand Interest

---

## CAM3 – Entrance Camera

### Coverage

* Store entrance
* Store exit

### Business Purpose

Primary visitor counting camera.

### Observable Events

* Visitor Entry
* Visitor Exit
* Re-entry Detection

---

## CAM4 – Backroom / Restricted Area

### Coverage

* Inventory room
* Staff-only area

### Business Purpose

Security and operational monitoring.

### Observable Events

* Unauthorized Access
* After-hours Activity
* Staff Presence

---

## CAM5 – Skincare Zone

### Coverage

* Face Shop
* Good Vibes
* Derma Co
* Minimalist
* Central Product Displays

### Business Purpose

Measure skincare engagement.

### Observable Events

* Zone Entry
* Zone Exit
* Dwell Time

---

# Blind Spots

## CAM1

* Under POS counter
* Areas hidden by customers

## CAM2

* Behind display shelves

## CAM3

* Exterior mall corridor beyond entrance

## CAM4

* Areas behind storage racks

## CAM5

* Areas blocked by promotional displays

---

# Operational Insights

1. Customers generally enter through CAM3.
2. Customers browse makeup and skincare sections.
3. Some customers proceed to checkout.
4. Staff move frequently between retail floor and backroom.
5. Queue buildup occurs near checkout during busy periods.
