# Business Metrics Specification

## Objective

Define all business KPIs generated by the ReIntellect Store Intelligence Platform.

The goal is to transform raw CCTV events into actionable retail analytics.

---

# Primary KPI

## Conversion Rate

Definition:

Conversion Rate = Purchasers / Unique Visitors

Formula:

conversion_rate =
(total_purchases / total_unique_visitors) * 100

Data Sources:

* Visitor count from CAM3
* Purchases from POS transaction data

Business Value:

Measures how effectively visitors are converted into paying customers.

---

# Visitor Metrics

## Unique Visitors

Definition:

Number of distinct visitors entering the store.

Source:

CAM3 Entry Zone

---

## Repeat Visitors

Definition:

Visitors detected entering the store multiple times within a configurable time window.

Source:

Tracking System

---

## Visitor Traffic By Hour

Definition:

Visitor count grouped by hour.

Source:

CAM3 Entry Events

---

# Engagement Metrics

## Zone Visits

Definition:

Number of visitors entering a specific brand zone.

Examples:

* Maybelline Visits
* Lakme Visits
* Faces Canada Visits
* Swiss Beauty Visits
* Face Shop Visits
* Good Vibes Visits

Source:

Zone Entry Events

---

## Dwell Time

Definition:

Time spent inside a zone.

Formula:

dwell_time =
zone_exit_time - zone_enter_time

Metrics:

* Average Dwell Time
* Maximum Dwell Time
* Total Dwell Time

Source:

Zone Events

---

## Most Engaged Brand

Definition:

Brand zone with highest average dwell time.

Source:

Zone Analytics

---

# Checkout Metrics

## Queue Length

Definition:

Current number of visitors inside checkout queue.

Source:

QUEUE_JOIN and QUEUE_LEAVE events

---

## Average Wait Time

Definition:

Average time between queue join and checkout start.

Source:

Queue Events

---

## Queue Abandonment Rate

Definition:

Percentage of visitors leaving queue before checkout.

Formula:

abandonment_rate =
(queue_abandonments / queue_entries) * 100

Source:

Queue Events

---

## Checkout Duration

Definition:

Time spent interacting with cashier.

Source:

POS Interaction Events

---

# Revenue Metrics

## Revenue

Definition:

Total sales value.

Source:

POS Data

---

## Revenue By Hour

Definition:

Revenue grouped by hour.

Source:

POS Data

---

## Average Basket Value

Definition:

Average transaction amount.

Formula:

average_basket_value =
total_revenue / total_transactions

Source:

POS Data

---

## Top Selling Brands

Definition:

Brands generating highest revenue.

Source:

POS Data

---

# Staff Metrics

## Cashier Utilization

Definition:

Percentage of operating time cashier station is occupied.

Source:

Cashier Presence Events

---

## POS Idle Time

Definition:

Time checkout station remains unattended.

Source:

Cashier Presence Events

---

# Security Metrics

## Restricted Area Access

Definition:

Number of entries into staff-only zones.

Source:

Backroom Camera

---

## After Hours Activity

Definition:

Movement detected outside configured store hours.

Source:

Backroom Camera
